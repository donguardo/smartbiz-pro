# First-visit intro video

## Build
- Upload the provided portrait MP4 as an app media asset.
- Add a full-screen intro that autoplays muted on a visitor’s first visit.
- Keep the video proportionally fitted against the pitch-black background on phones and desktops.
- Add an always-visible **Skip** control and close the intro automatically when playback ends.
- Remember completion in the browser so returning visitors go straight to the site.
- Delay the install prompt until the intro has finished or been skipped, preventing overlapping pop-ups.

## Verification
- Confirm first-visit playback, skipping, automatic completion, and returning-visit behavior in the preview.
- Check phone and desktop layouts and confirm the app remains error-free.
